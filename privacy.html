Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

rawshutterimages ("we", "us", "our") operates the Phonics Play & Learn application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

Phonics Play & Learn is designed with privacy as a priority. We store the following information locally on your device (and, on iPhone and iPad, in a copy in your own iCloud account, described below):

  • Player profile names and avatar selections
  • Lesson progress and scores
  • Game statistics and achievements
  • App settings and preferences
  • Subscription status

None of this data is sent to us or to any third party. We do not operate any servers.

2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Phonics Play & Learn is designed for children and we take children's privacy very seriously:

  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.
  • We do not require children to provide any personal information to use the App.
  • Player names chosen during profile creation are stored on the device and, on iPhone and iPad, in your own iCloud copy. We never see them.
  • We do not use advertising or behavioural tracking of any kind.
  • We do not share any data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • All parental controls are protected by a parent gate.

3. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used by Phonics Play & Learn is the payment platform of the store you downloaded the App from:

  • Apple In-App Purchase (StoreKit) on iOS/iPadOS: Used solely to process subscription payments. Apple handles all payment information according to their own privacy policy. We do not have access to your payment details.
  • Google Play Billing on Android: Used solely to process subscription payments. Google handles all payment information according to the Google Play Terms of Service and Google's privacy policy. We do not have access to your payment details.

We do not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media integrations
  • Crash reporting services
  • Any other third-party SDKs that collect user data

4. Data Storage & Security

  • All app data is stored privately on your device, inside the App's own storage area, which other apps cannot read.
  • No data is stored on our servers; we do not have any. The only copy away from the device is the automatic one in your own iCloud account (iPhone and iPad), described below.
  • No internet connection is required for the app to function (except for initial subscription purchase/restoration).

Backing up and restoring progress

  • Automatic iCloud copy (iPhone and iPad): each time the App is closed, it saves a copy of each player profile (name and emoji avatar) and that player's progress, stars, rewards, streak and learning-path position to your own iCloud account, using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. It belongs to your Apple ID and only your devices signed in to it can read it; we cannot see or access it. On a new iPhone or iPad signed in to the same Apple ID, the App offers to restore it. It does not contain payment or subscription details. If you are not signed in to iCloud, nothing is saved there.
  • Manual backup (in the Parent area): a grown-up can export a backup file. It contains each player profile (the name and emoji avatar chosen for it) and that player's progress, stars, rewards, streak and learning-path position. It does not contain payment or subscription details.
  • The backup file is saved on your device. On iPhone and iPad it appears in the Files app under the App's folder. You decide whether to copy it anywhere else, such as iCloud Drive or a computer. The App never sends it to us or to anyone else.
  • Restoring: a backup file placed back in that folder can be restored from the Parent area. Restoring adds or replaces the profiles in the file and leaves other profiles on the device alone.
  • Device backups: like most apps, the App's data may be included in your device's own backups, such as iCloud or computer backups on Apple devices or Android's automatic backup to your Google account. These are controlled by Apple or Google and your device settings, not by us, and we cannot access them.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all app data at any time by:

  • Removing individual player profiles within the App, which also removes that profile's iCloud copy
  • Removing the App's iCloud data in your device's Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage
  • Uninstalling the App from your device, which removes all locally stored data (any backup files you have saved, and your device's own backups, are kept until you delete them)

6. Subscription Data

When you subscribe to Phonics Play & Learn Premium:

  • Your payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (on iOS/iPadOS) or by Google through Google Play (on Android).
  • We store only your subscription status (active/inactive) and period (monthly/annual) locally on your device.
  • We do not have access to your Apple ID or Google account, payment method, or billing information.

7. No Tracking or Analytics

Phonics Play & Learn does not:

  • Track your location
  • Access your contacts, photos, or other device data
  • Use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • Collect device identifiers for advertising
  • Profile users for any purpose

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy.

9. Parental Rights

As a parent or guardian, you have the right to:

  • Review the information stored in your child's profile
  • Delete your child's profile and all associated data
  • Control your child's access to the App through the parent gate
